## 4. Academic Structure & Streams Offered
Sant G.D.S. Inter College caters to students from upper primary/middle grades up to Intermediate (Class 12th) under the UPMSP curriculum:

### High School (Classes 9 & 10)
* **Core Curriculum:**
  * Hindi (General & Sahityik)
  * English
  * Mathematics (गणित)
  * Science (विज्ञान — Physics, Chemistry, Biology basics)
  * Social Science (सामाजिक विज्ञान — History, Civics, Geography, Economics)

  * General Hindi & English


 **Medium of Instruction:** Hindi with English language options in accordance with UP Board standards.
