How to Reach the College

Conveniently situated on the Khandauli-Dharera-Etmadpur road in Agra district.

Transit Landmarks
  • From Khandauli: Approx. 5-7 km via Khandauli-Dharera Road.
  • From Etmadpur: Approx. 8 km connecting to Dharera Mauja.

  • Shared tempos and local buses regularly ply through Dharera stop.
