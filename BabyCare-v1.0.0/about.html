OUR HERITAGE & VISION

Sant G.D.S. Inter College, Dharera, Agra

Established to bring top-standard secondary and higher secondary education to students residing in Dharera, Khandauli, and Etmadpur tehsils of Agra, Sant G.D.S. Inter College (संत जी.डी.एस. इण्टर कॉलेज) stands as a pillar of disciplined academic development.

Affiliated with the renowned Uttar Pradesh Madhyamik Shiksha Parishad (UPMSP), Prayagraj under official School Code 1820 and UDISE Code 09150600131, the college is officially recognized as an examination centre and educational hub in Agra District (Code 01).

CURRICULUM ARCHITECTURE

Academics & Subject Streams

Rigorous syllabi prescribed by Uttar Pradesh Madhyamik Shiksha Parishad (UPMSP).

Classes 11 & 12

Intermediate (Science Stream)

Designed for aspiring doctors, engineers, researchers, and scientific professionals:

  • Physics (भौतिक विज्ञान): Mechanics, Thermodynamics, Optics & Modern Physics with practical lab work.
  • Chemistry (रसायन विज्ञान): Inorganic, Organic, Physical Chemistry & analytical titration experiments.
  • Mathematics (गणित) / Biology (जीव विज्ञान): Calculus, Algebra & Coordinate Geometry OR Botany & Zoology practical studies.
  • Languages: General Hindi (सामान्य हिन्दी) & English grammar and literature.
Classes 9 & 10

High School (Foundation)

Forming a solid base in fundamental disciplines for high board examination scores:

  • Mathematics (गणित): Comprehensive foundation in arithmetic, geometry, trigonometry and statistics.
  • Science (विज्ञान): Integrated fundamentals of Physics, Chemistry, and Life Sciences.
  • Social Science (सामाजिक विज्ञान): History, Civics, Geography and Economics.
  • Hindi & English: Language comprehension, essay writing, and grammar fluency.
HOLISTIC DEVELOPMENT

Nurturing Cultural Values & Physical Wellness

Education at Sant G.D.S. Inter College goes beyond textbook theory. We hold daily open-air Yoga & Meditation sessions to cultivate focus, stamina, and mental peace in students preparing for competitive life.

National festivals like Independence Day, Republic Day, Gandhi Jayanti, and Saraswati Puja are observed with utmost devotion and student participation, instilling deep patriotism and respect for Indian traditions.
